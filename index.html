Work in Progress

Who Cares? Parental Leave Benefits and the Household Division of Childcare

with Federica Meluzzi and Jakob Wegmann

We use a double regression kink design to study how the monetary generosity of parental leave benefits shapes the intra-household allocation of childcare, drawing on administrative data covering millions of German households.
